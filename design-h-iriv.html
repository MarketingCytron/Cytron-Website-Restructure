Cytron IRIV Series

Industrial Controllers Built on Raspberry Pi

One family of industrial controllers, edge AI units and display hubs, designed by Cytron for factories, farms and buildings.

Built on Raspberry Pi

Raspberry Pi CM4 and CM5 inside. Linux, Python, Node-RED and Docker, with no locked toolchain.

Ready for the Panel

Metal DIN-rail enclosures with industrial I/O and RS485, made for cabinets and control panels.

Designed in Penang

By Cytron, the first Raspberry Pi Design Partner in Southeast Asia, with in-house design and manufacturing.

Local Support

Engineers in Malaysia, step-by-step tutorials, HRDC-claimable training and a 12-month warranty.
