# Archive: design options not chosen

The chosen design for the my.cytron.io revamp is **Design D**, at the root of this repository.
This folder keeps the other options for reference. They still work but are not maintained.

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `design-c/` | Design C, a new page layout in the Design D look, with an audience chooser on the Store page. Store, Education, Industry and Product pages, with its own copy of `site.css`, `site.js`, `pdp.css` and `pdp.js`. |
| `design-e/` | Design E, layout ideas from apple.com/my (full-width product tiles, thin dark menu bar, media galleries). Store, Education, Industry and Product pages, with `design-e.css`, `design-e.js` and `pdp.js`. |

Live links: `archive/` on the GitHub Pages site lists every page.

Archived on 15 Oct 2026.
