Simplifying ESP32 for beginners.

The Maker ESP32 is designed to take the frustration out of building smart electronics and IoT projects. While standard development boards leave you guessing when code fails, this board gives you instant visual and audio feedback right out of the box. Whether you are writing your first lines of code, teaching a classroom, or prototyping a connected device, the Maker ESP32 helps you understand what your board is doing at every step.

With plug-and-play sensor ports, onboard diagnostic lights, and flexible power options, you can focus on bringing your ideas to life without fighting messy jumper wires or complex troubleshooting setups. It also shares a familiar standard pin layout, making it fully compatible with expansion boards like the Robo ESP32 and the NodeMCU ESP32 Expansion Board for easy upgrade and seamless hardware integration.

Get the highlights.

Built to show you what your code is doing.

01See Your Code in Action

Onboard status LEDs light up instantly to show pin activity and serial data transfer, letting you verify signal flow without needing extra diagnostic gear.

02Plug-and-Play Expansion

Connect I²C sensors directly using the Maker Port for fast, solderless prototyping compatible with popular sensor ecosystems.

03Instant Sound & Control

Includes a built-in user button for custom triggers and an onboard buzzer for audio feedback, complete with a physical mute switch when you need to code in silence.

04Expanded Code Capacity

Powered by an upgraded ESP32-WROOM module that provides extra flash memory space for larger codebases, local web servers, and over-the-air (OTA) wireless updates.

05Seamless Board Compatibility

Designed around the standard NodeMCU footprint so you can easily drop it into existing projects, codebases, and expansion shields.

06Modern Power Delivery

Powers and programs via a sturdy, reversible USB Type-C connector, eliminating orientation hassle while delivering reliable power to onboard and external components.
