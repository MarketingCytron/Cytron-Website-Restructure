Power meters and inverters already speak RS485 Modbus. Collect their readings, send them to a dashboard and spot waste or faults early.

  • Read meters and inverters over RS485 Modbus, or clamp on wireless LoRaWAN power sensors
  • Live dashboards and alerts over 4G or Ethernet
  • Data for ESG reporting and maintenance planning
