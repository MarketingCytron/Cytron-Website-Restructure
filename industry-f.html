FAQ

Questions we often hear

Is Raspberry Pi suitable for industrial use?

Yes. The Raspberry Pi Compute Module 5 uses on-board eMMC storage instead of an SD card, is rated from −20 °C to +85 °C, and stays in production until at least January 2036. IRIV controllers add isolated I/O, RS485, a 10–30 V DC supply and a metal DIN-rail enclosure.

Do you deliver complete solutions?

We focus on hardware and technical support. When you need a complete, installed solution, we connect you with a system integrator who delivers it using our hardware.

Can you design a custom board for our company?

Yes. As a Raspberry Pi Design Partner, our engineers design, fabricate and test custom boards, then supply your repeat orders. We start with a discussion of your spec and use. Minimum quantities apply.

Do you sell other brands as well as IRIV?

Yes. We supply partner brands such as Teltonika, Milesight, Seeed Studio and EDATEC, so you can source a whole project from one supplier.

Which motors do your motor drivers support?

Brushed DC motors, from low voltage up to 58 V depending on the model. They are not for AC motors.

Are your trainings HRDC claimable?

Yes. All our industry trainings and workshops are HRDC claimable.

What warranty do IRIV products have?

IRIV controllers come with a 12-month warranty, with support from our team in Malaysia.

Do you serve customers outside Malaysia?

Yes. Cytron has stores for Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Vietnam, and an international store.
