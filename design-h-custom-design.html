Raspberry Pi Design Partner, first in Southeast Asia

Cytron Technologies has achieved the status of Raspberry Pi Design Partner. As the first company in Southeast Asia to achieve this status, we’re excited to provide even better solutions for our industrial clients in this region using Raspberry Pi products.

About the partnership

What We Do

Two Services, One Team

Design Service, Based on Raspberry Pi

Custom controllers and carrier boards built around Raspberry Pi products, by the team behind IRIV PiControl and the CM4 Maker Board.

PCB Assembly Service

PCB assembly customized to your needs, on the same lines that build Cytron’s own products.

How It Works

From Idea to Production

We can join at any stage: proof of concept, pre-commercialization, or a product already in market.

  1. 1Tell us your idea

    Share what you are building, the stage you are at and the volume you expect.

  2. 2Design with Raspberry Pi

    Our engineers design around Raspberry Pi products as a Raspberry Pi Design Partner.

  3. 3Prototype and refine

    Test the first units with you and adjust quickly, the agile way.

  4. 4Manufacture

    PCB assembly customised to your needs, from pilot runs to mass production.
